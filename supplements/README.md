# Supplementary materials

This directory contains the materials used or described by the paper and its formal supplement.

## Appendix

- [Paper supplement](appendix/paper-supplement.pdf): Formal paper supplement.
- [Pattern and carrier review](appendix/pattern-carrier-review.xlsx): Review workbook covering pattern and carrier observations, cross-corpus comparisons, and within-corpus summaries.

## Human validation

- [Rating form](validation/rating-form.xlsx): Blank English form with instructions and the fixed 163-item sample.
- [Validation items](validation/validation-items.csv): Sentences, rated protein pairs, proposed triggers and carriers, and sampling fields.
- [Validation ratings](validation/validation-ratings-2026-09-12.csv): Anonymous, comment-free ratings collected on 2026-09-12.
- [Validation notes](validation/README.txt): Rating definitions, scoring rules, and validation commands.

## Analysis data

- **`ledgers/<corpus>/pair_observations.csv`**: Pair-level observations used by the paper analyses.
- **`ledgers/<corpus>/pattern_observations.csv`**: Pattern-level observations.
- **`ledgers/<corpus>/carrier_trigger_observations.csv`**: Carrier and trigger observations.
- [Cross-corpus results](results/cross-corpus/): Pattern rates and Fisher tests.
- [Ablation results](results/ablation/): QANom and coreference ablation results.
- [Within-corpus cells](results/within-corpus-cells.csv): Within-corpus comparison cells.
- [Within-corpus summary](results/within-corpus-summary.csv): Summary reported in the paper.
- [Core-physical cells](results/within-corpus-physical-cells.csv): Core-physical comparison cells.
- [Within-corpus null](results/within-corpus-null.json): Within-corpus label-shuffle null result.
- [Construction families](results/construction-families.json): Construction-family summary.
- [Coverage](results/coverage.json): Evidence-coverage audit.
- [Matched-gap nulls](results/matched-gap-nulls.json): Matched-gap null analyses.
- [Residual corpus effect](results/residual-corpus.json): Residual corpus-effect analysis.
- [Scope sensitivity](results/scope-sensitivity.json): Inclusion-rule sensitivity analysis.
- [Vocabulary control](results/vocabulary-control.json): Vocabulary-control analysis.
